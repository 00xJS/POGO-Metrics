01How it works

Three steps, about ten minutes of your time, and one ~7-day wait on Niantic. No coding, no spreadsheets — and you can stop at any single file.

STEP 1

Ask Niantic for your data

Every player has the right to a full copy of their account data. You request it once and it arrives as a download link, usually within a week.

STEP 2

Drop in your files

Unzip the export and drag in the whole folder — or just the one file you're curious about, like FriendList.tsv. Upload one, some, or all of them.

STEP 3

Meet your metrics — then play your story

Each file you add lights up a new chapter — your activity rhythm, your map, your friendships, your spending. Then hit ▶ Play my story for a full-screen, tappable recap of your whole trainer life, and download a card to share.

02Get your export from Niantic

This is a free, official right you have under privacy laws like GDPR and CCPA — not a hack or a third-party tool. Here's the quickest path.

  1. Open Pokémon GO and tap the Poké Ball. That opens the main menu. Tap the Settings gear in the top-right.
  2. Tap Help (top-right), then “Chat with us.” Start a New Conversation with the support assistant.
  3. Choose “My account,” then “Request my data.” It will ask whether your account is a kids login (answer yes/no).
  4. Confirm the request. An automated message asks you to confirm — tap Continue to submit your data request.
  5. Wait up to 7 days for their reply. Niantic responds with a download link (valid for 7 days) and a password to open the file.
  6. Unzip it — this is the step that trips people up. The download is a password-protected ZIP, and the password is in Niantic's message. On a computer: double-click it and type the password — Windows and macOS both handle this. On an iPhone or iPad: the Files app cannot open a password-protected ZIP, so email or AirDrop the file to a computer, or install a ZIP app that supports passwords. Inside you'll find .tsv, .csv, .txt and .json files — exactly what this site reads. Then hit Visualize my journey.
Tip: the export is a snapshot in time. Some files (like your GPS trail or fitness data) only cover a short recent window, so the sooner you request after a big trip or event, the richer that part of your story will be. Not ready? See a live example first →
Just sent your request? The wait is up to a week — and the download link Niantic sends expires after 7 days. 📅 Download a calendar reminder to check back, and bookmark this page now so your story is one tap away.

03Privacy, by design

Your export is intimate — it can describe where you live, who you know, and what you spend. By design, this was built so you never have to upload or share any of it.

🖥️

It all runs locally

Parsing and charts happen entirely in your browser with JavaScript. There is no backend to send your files to — close the tab and it's gone.

🙈

We ignore the scary bits

Your export also holds your email, IP addresses and advertising IDs. Nothing here ever shows them, charts them, or writes them to a file — the visualisations only touch the parts that tell a story.

📤

Your map is the exception

Your locations are mapped — that's the whole point of the globe. It's drawn on your device and published nowhere, but it does show where you play, so give any screenshot a look before you share it.
