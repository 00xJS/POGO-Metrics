01How it works

Three steps, about ten minutes of your time, and one wait on support — a day to a few weeks. No coding, no spreadsheets — and you can stop at any single file.

STEP 1

Ask for your data

Every player has the right to a full copy of their account data. You request it once, in-game, and it arrives as a download link — within a day under Niantic, and up to a few weeks since Scopely took over the game.

STEP 2

Drop in your files

Unzip the download and drag in the whole folder — the Player_Journey.zip inside it is opened for you — or just the one file you're curious about, like FriendList.tsv. Add one, some, or all of them.

STEP 3

Meet your metrics — then play your story

Each file you add lights up a new chapter — your activity rhythm, your map, your friendships, your spending. Then hit Play my story for a full-screen, tappable recap, and download a card to share.

Getting the export — the quickest path

A free, official right under privacy laws like GDPR and CCPA — not a hack or a third-party tool. Four screens in the game, then one email. Scopely runs Pokémon GO now, but the screens, the automated replies and the files are the same ones Niantic built.

Unzip it — this is the step that trips people up. The download is a password-protected ZIP, and the password is in the message. On a computer: double-click it and type the password — Windows and macOS both handle this. On an iPhone or iPad: the Files app cannot open a password-protected ZIP, so email or AirDrop the file to a computer, or install a ZIP app that supports passwords. Inside you'll find .tsv, .csv, .txt and .json files — exactly what this site reads — plus a second archive, Player_Journey.zip, which has no password: leave it be, it's opened for you when you drop the folder. Since the Scopely handover the folder carries a long random ID instead of “Pokemon GO Data”, which is normal. Then hit Visualize my journey.
Tip: the export is a snapshot in time. Some files (like your GPS trail or fitness data) only cover a short recent window, so the sooner you request after a big trip or event, the richer that part of your story will be. Not ready? See a live example first →
Just sent your request? The wait has run from a day (Niantic, June 2026) to about four weeks (Scopely, August 2026) — and the download link expires 7 days after it lands. to check back, and bookmark this page now so your story is one tap away.

02Privacy, by design

Your export is intimate — it can describe where you live, who you know, and what you spend. By design, this was built so you never have to upload or share any of it.

It all runs locally

Parsing and charts happen entirely in your browser with JavaScript. There is no backend to send your files to — close the tab and it's gone.

We ignore the scary bits

Your export also holds your email, IP addresses and advertising IDs. Nothing here ever shows them, charts them, or writes them to a file — the visualizations only touch the parts that tell a story.

Your map is the exception

Your locations are mapped — that's the whole point of the globe. It's drawn on your device and published nowhere, but it does show where you play, so give any screenshot a look before you share it.
